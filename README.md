# Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis Using Python

## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data to understand sales trends, regional performance, product profitability, and the relationship between discounts and profit.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab

## Project Objectives
- Analyze yearly and monthly sales trends.
- Compare sales and profit across product categories and regions.
- Identify the most profitable and lowest-profit products.
- Examine the relationship between discount levels and average profit.

## Key Business Findings

1. Yearly Sales Growth: Sales increased from 484,247.50 in 2014 to 733,215.26 in 2017, representing approximately 51.4% growth.
2. Regional Performance: The West region recorded the highest sales (725,457.82) and profit (108,418.45).
3. Top Profitable Product: The Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier generated the highest total product profit among the products analyzed, at 25,199.93.
4. Loss-Making Product: The Cubify CubeX 3D Printer Double Head Print recorded the largest total loss among the products analyzed, at -8,879.97.
5. Sales vs. Profit: The Central region generated 501,239.89 in sales but only 39,706.36 in profit, highlighting the importance of evaluating profitability alongside sales.

## Business Recommendations

- Study the West region's performance to identify practices that could benefit other regions.
- Investigate the Central region's profitability by examining costs, discounts, and product mix.
- Review pricing, costs, and discounts for loss-making products.
- Monitor sales and profit together when evaluating business performance.

## Conclusion
This project provided practical experience in data cleaning, data aggregation, exploratory data analysis, and data visualization using Python. It also demonstrated how sales data can be used to identify business patterns and areas for further investigation.
